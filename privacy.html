Privacy Policy

Tech Validation. Effective 13 September 2026.

Who operates these tools

Tech Validation is a set of personal tools built and operated by one person for their own use on their own computer. There are no other users, no accounts to create, and nothing is sold or offered as a service.

What Google data the tools access

  • The email address of the signed-in Google account, used only to confirm that the tools are connected to the owner's own account.
  • Files in the owner's Google Drive, limited to the folders the owner designates, for example a folder where the owner drops voice memos from a phone.
  • Google Docs that the owner chooses, to append the owner's own notes.

Access is granted by the owner through Google's consent screen and can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How the data is used

Downloaded files are stored on the owner's own computer. Audio recordings are transcribed by sending the audio to OpenAI's transcription API; the resulting text is kept locally and used to organize the owner's work. Backup copies of the owner's local data may be uploaded to the owner's own Google Drive.

The tools never share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone else, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train machine-learning models. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Revoking the tools' access in the Google account settings stops all further access immediately; nothing new is retrieved after that.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
